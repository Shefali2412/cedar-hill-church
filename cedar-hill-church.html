Services & ministries

Worship with us

Every gathering is open to everyone. Can't make it in person? Sunday services stream live at 11:00 AM.

Sunday Worship

Contemporary music, a sermon from our current series and communion on the first Sunday of each month.

Cedar Kids

Bible stories, songs and small groups for ages 0 to 11, led by background-checked volunteers.

An hour in the Word with open discussion. This fall we are reading through the Gospel of John.
